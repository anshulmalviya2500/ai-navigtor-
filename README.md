# AI Career Navigator

> An AI-powered career guidance platform that helps students discover suitable career paths, assess their skills, compare opportunities, and follow structured learning roadmaps.

**[Visit AI Career Navigator](https://onebyai.vercel.app/)**

## Overview

**AI Career Navigator** is a modern career exploration platform built with **Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Google Gemini AI**.

The platform helps users understand which career paths align with their **skills, interests, and strengths**. It combines AI-generated recommendations with curated career information and structured learning roadmaps to provide a complete career-planning experience.
